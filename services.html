BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Where should we start?

Start with the business challenge. If the positioning is unclear, strategy may come first. If the direction is established, we can discuss identity or campaign work.

Can you work with an existing brand?

The service offering includes brand experience and refresh work. Tell us which elements you want to retain and what needs to change.

How long will the project take?

Timing depends on the scope, number of deliverables and review process. We’ll discuss those before agreeing a project schedule.
