This is a local concept, separate from the live WordPress site. Page copy and deliverable descriptions are proposals for business approval.

Confirm the current operating locations, service area, contact details and approved project descriptions. No unverified performance metrics or testimonials appear in the pages.

The form creates an email draft for review. Production deployment should connect an approved form service and add privacy information, spam protection and delivery checks.

The HTML includes noindex metadata to discourage accidental indexing. Keep the assets folder beside the pages. External source links and an email app require their usual connectivity.
